# 🏛️ Ward-Watch

A Citizen Complaint & Local Government Transparency Platform

Starting at the Municipality Level. Scaling to Districts, Provinces, Nationwide, and Beyond.
Ward-Watch is designed as a geographically scalable platform that initially focuses on municipalities and rural municipalities in Nepal. With its flexible and hierarchical architecture, the platform can gradually expand to district-level administration, provincial governments, nationwide implementation, and potentially international markets in the future.

Our vision is to build a unified platform that promotes citizen participation, transparent governance, and public accountability across geographical boundaries.

Ward-Watch is a two-sided web platform that connects citizens with local government authorities across Nepal. It enables citizens to report local issues, track complaint progress, and monitor ward-level development projects and Public Budgets.

Our goal is to make local governance more transparent, accountable, and citizen-friendly.

# 🌟The Problem 

Citizens in municipalities and rural municipalities across Nepal face problems such as:

- Broken roads and damaged infrastructure.

- Water shortages and poor sanitation.

- Unmanaged waste and drainage problems.

- Lack of transparency in local development budgets.

- No proper system to track submitted complaints.

- Delayed responses without clear accountability.

Traditional systems often rely on paper forms and phone calls, making it difficult for citizens to monitor progress or know whether their problems have been addressed.
Existing digital systems often lack transparency in public budget spending, project progress, contractor details, and responsible officials. Citizens also lack a unified platform to report local issues, track complaints, and monitor their resolution.

# 💡 Our Solution

- Ward-Watch provides a centralized digital platform where citizens can report local issues and government officials can manage them efficiently.

- The platform follows Nepal's administrative hierarchy:

- Province → District → Municipality → Ward

- Ward-Watch provides separate dashboards for citizens, ward officials, and municipal administrators, along with a public transparency dashboard accessible to everyone. Citizens can track their complaints, view government responses, and monitor the complete development process, including budget allocation and expenditure, assigned contractors, project deadlines, and regular progress updates. This helps citizens verify whether projects are completed on time and within the allocated budget.


# ✨ Key Features

## 👤 For Citizens

- Secure registration and login.

- Select province, district, municipality, and ward.

- Submit complaints with category and description.

- Upload photos as evidence.

- Pin the exact complaint location on an interactive map.

- Track complaints through a live status timeline.

- Receive updates about complaint progress.


## 🏢 For Ward Officials

- Secure role-based admin dashboard.

- View complaints submitted to their ward.

- Assign and manage complaints.

- Update complaint status:

- Submitted ---> Assigned ---> In Progress --->Resolved

- Upload proof of completed work like live Photo and live Short Videos

- Manage ward development projects and budgets.

- Automatically escalate overdue complaints to higher authorities.


## 🏛️ For Municipal Administrators

- Municipality-wide complaint management.

- Monitor complaints across all wards.

- Filter complaints by ward and category.

- View ward performance rankings.

- Monitor municipality-wide statistics.

- Create and manage ward official accounts.


## 🌍 Public Transparency Dashboard

- No login required.

- View ward-wise budget allocation and utilization.

- Monitor development project progress.

- Explore complaints through an interactive map.

- Compare ward performance scores.
 
- Track complaint resolution and overdue rates.


## 📊 Ward Performance Score

- Each ward receives a performance score based on:

- Complaint resolution rate.

- Overdue complaint rate.

- Budget utilization.

- This helps citizens understand how effectively their local government is performing.
