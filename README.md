# 🏛️ Ward-Watch

A Citizen Complaint & Local Government Transparency Platform

Starting at the Municipality Level. Scaling to Districts, Provinces, Nationwide, and Beyond.
Ward-Watch is designed as a geographically scalable platform that initially focuses on municipalities and rural municipalities in Nepal. With its flexible and hierarchical architecture, the platform can gradually expand to district-level administration, provincial governments, nationwide implementation, and potentially international markets in the future.

Our vision is to build a unified platform that promotes citizen participation, transparent governance, and public accountability across geographical boundaries.

Ward-Watch is a two-sided web platform that connects citizens with local government authorities across Nepal. It enables citizens to report local issues, track complaint progress, and monitor ward-level development projects and Public Budgets.

Our goal is to make local governance more transparent, accountable, and citizen-friendly.

# 🌟The Problem 

Citizens in municipalities and rural municipalities across Nepal face problems such as:

- Broken roads and damaged infrastructure.

- Water shortages and poor sanitation.

- Unmanaged waste and drainage problems.

- Lack of transparency in local development budgets.

- No proper system to track submitted complaints.

- Delayed responses without clear accountability.

Traditional complaint systems often rely on paper forms and phone calls, making it difficult for citizens to monitor progress or know whether their problems have been addressed.
